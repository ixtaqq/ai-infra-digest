## Highlights

- `/digest` and `/last` now retrieve the published briefing without generating another AI edition or consuming a scheduled delivery slot. Watchlist modes and explicit filters apply consistently.
- The [briefing page](https://goldirham-stack.vercel.app/briefing/) shows the latest edition, linked sources, counterarguments, and dated company coverage, thesis history, and prices.
- Alert and price-watch delivery now holds uncertain sends for review instead of automatically replaying them. Invalid or stale quotes cannot be presented as current prices.
- Personal settings are limited to private Telegram chats. Webhook updates are stored and deduplicated before acknowledgement. Editorial generation claims prevent duplicate runs for an existing edition.
- The research dashboard uses complete feed status and date-range stock aggregates. Vulnerable dependencies were updated, and the production container runs on Node 22 as an unprivileged user.

## Deployment and verification

The server, four additive database migrations, and website were deployed on September 24, 2026. The production briefing and dashboard loaded saved data, public reads were limited to approved tables, and unauthenticated webhook requests were rejected.

The release passed 483 application tests, 119 database checks, local HTTP and concurrent-claim checks, responsive browser verification, CodeQL, and a dependency audit with zero reported vulnerabilities. The [rollout record](https://github.com/ixtaqq/ai-infra-digest/blob/main/docs/roadmap-implementation.md) describes the checks and recovery steps.

## Remaining limits

Real-recipient delivery, current SMTP and Slack credentials, a restored-backup drill, and editorial accuracy against a human-reviewed benchmark remain to be verified. Price watches are sampled at delivery time; they are not real-time crossing alerts. Enforced AI spending limits require reviewed provider prices before enabling them.
